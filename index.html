Equip a skill to Q or E. Skills level up when they land: faster windup, shorter freeze, more damage. Attach a mantra to change how a skill behaves.

Clash Trial

Swing into their swing right as it releases and the blades collide. Keep winning the timing and it becomes an exchange. Your click rhythm picks the move: pause mid-combo for a guard crush or a rising cut. Spam the same thing and it loses power, and they learn it.
